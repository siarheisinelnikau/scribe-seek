Scribe Seek's first public release turns audio and video recordings into searchable transcripts on your computer.

### Included

- Local Whisper recognition with Tiny, Base and Small Q5 model downloads.
- Transcript search with highlighted matches, timestamp playback, waveform seeking and playback speed controls.
- TXT, SRT, VTT and JSON export, with a persistent local recording library.
- English as the default interface, plus Russian, French, German, Spanish and Italian localizations.
- Settings that show model and data folders, with buttons to open them.
- Background startup update checks, an opt-out toggle, manual checking and verified in-app installation with restart and a rollback copy.
- Compact controls and sidebar layout fixes for smaller windows.
